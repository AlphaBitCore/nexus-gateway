# PRE-GATE — NOT PUBLISHABLE

All `benchmark/v2` result artifacts are internal historical material.

They predate the v3 mandatory validation pipeline and must not be used for:

- public claims
- cross-gateway rankings
- throughput comparisons
- latency comparisons
- marketing material
- client materials

They remain retained for audit/history only.

New numbers come only from `benchmark/v3` (Story A observatory or Story B rig
importer), which enforces open-loop load, complete outcome accounting, pinned
versions, non-burstable compute, verified upstream, and symmetric validation. See
`../../v3/README.md` and `../RETIRED_MEASUREMENT_CORE.md`.
